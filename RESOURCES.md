# Engine Hire Resources

## Knowledge

- [Engine Hire: Staffing Agency App](https://enginehire.io/app)
  Worker/candidate and client app features and claimed benefits. Use for: what workers actually get (shift accept, clock-in, geofencing, ratings). Vendor marketing: treat numbers as claims.
- [Engine Hire: Contract Staffing Software](https://enginehire.io/contract-staffing-software)
  The five product modules (scheduling, compliance, payroll/billing, portals, analytics). Use for: mapping features to value levers.
- [Engine Hire: Role of Temporary Staffing App](https://enginehire.io/rise-on-demand-staffing-role-temp-staffing-apps/)
  Value claims per audience (workers, agencies, clients). Use for: the three-sided value map.
- [Enginehire App on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/enginehire-app/id6444958739)
  Use for: the worker-facing experience and user reviews.

- [GetApp: Enginehire reviews](https://www.getapp.com/hr-employee-management-software/a/enginehire/reviews/)
  Independent listing, but only 5 reviews and little detail (one praises payment auto-reconciliation). Use for: weak corroboration only. G2 blocked automated access (403).

- [Beeple: Fill rate, the KPI that determines how much revenue your agency is missing](https://www.beeple.eu/en/post/fill-rate-the-kpi-that-determines-how-much-revenue-your-agency-is-missing-out-on)
  Fill rate formula and the denominator problem. Vendor blog (a competing staffing tool), so use for definitions only, not benchmarks.
- [GigSmart: Fill rate glossary](https://gigsmart.com/glossary/fill-rate)
  Short definitions of fill rate and related KPIs. Vendor glossary, same caution.

- [Intercom: RICE, Simple Prioritization for Product Managers](https://www.intercom.com/blog/rice-simple-prioritization-for-product-managers/)
  Original write-up of the RICE scoring framework (Reach, Impact, Confidence, Effort). Use for: ranking features by value per effort.

- [Statsig: Correlation does not mean causation in A/B tests](https://statsig.com/perspectives/ab-testing-correlation-causation-pitfalls)
  Why before/after comparisons mislead and why random assignment fixes it. Vendor blog (experimentation tool); use for the concepts only.
- [Spotify Confidence: What are confounding variables?](https://confidence.spotify.com/glossary/confounding-variables.md)
  Short definition of confounders. Use for: Lesson 7 vocabulary.
- [Enginehire App on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/my/app/enginehire-app/id6444958739) has no ratings or reviews yet ("not enough ratings"), so it can't corroborate worker value.

## Wisdom (Communities)

- [r/Recruitment](https://www.reddit.com/r/Recruitment/)
  Open to candidates, agency recruiters, in-house recruiters and recruiting-system developers, so you hear all sides. Use for: asking how agencies and workers experience scheduling, pay and no-shows. Moderation quality unchecked, and member counts come from an aggregator.
- [r/RecruitmentAgencies](https://www.reddit.com/r/RecruitmentAgencies/)
  Agency-owner perspective on running a staffing business. Use for: what agencies actually care about when buying software.

## Gaps
- Neutral benchmarks for fill rate, time-to-fill and no-show rate (current sources are vendors, so the lesson avoids benchmark numbers).
- Independent (non-vendor) evidence for the performance claims.
- Real worker/agency reviews (try G2 / GetApp listings).
- A neutral primer on staffing-agency economics (margin, time-to-fill).
- A moderated community of worker-side voices (temp and shift workers). Reddit's r/Recruitment includes candidates but is not worker-focused.
- A rigorous textbook-level source on causal inference for business (current ones are vendor blogs).
