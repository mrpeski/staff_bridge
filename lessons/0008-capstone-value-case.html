Capstone: a worker-first value case

Combine everything into one page. This is the proof that you can name the value, pitch it, measure it and rank what to build, which is the whole mission.

The brief

Write a one-page value case for Engine Hire, aimed at a skeptical agency owner who asks: "Why would my workers care, and how would I know it's working?" Use these six sections:

  1. Who: the three groups, in one line each (Lesson 1).
  2. Levers: choose the two that matter most to workers, and justify (Lesson 2).
  3. Pitch: 60 seconds, audience, pain, lever, evidence, test (Lesson 4).
  4. Metrics: one per chosen lever, with formulas (Lesson 3).
  5. Proof plan: baseline, comparison group, one confounder (Lesson 7).
  6. Next features: three ideas scored with RICE (Lesson 5).
