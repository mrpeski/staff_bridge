Prioritising features by lever

Every feature idea competes for limited effort. RICE gives a simple way to rank them, and your levers tell you what "impact" means.

RICE in one minute

Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort. Reach is people affected per period. Impact is per-person benefit on a scale of 3, 2, 1, 0.5, 0.25. Confidence is how sure you are (100%, 80%, 50%). Effort is person-months. A higher score is a better candidate, but the result is a guide, not a rule (Intercom).

Where levers fit. Before scoring, name the lever a feature moves and the metric from Lesson 3 it should shift. A feature with no lever or metric can't be given an honest Impact.

Practice

Why does RICE include a Confidence factor?

Per Intercom, confidence stops over-enthusiasm for untested ideas.

When might you ignore the top RICE score?

The framework calls itself a guide; dependencies and business requirements can justify deviating.
